Welcome to Sunrise Family Dental

Sunrise Family Dental has proudly served the families of north Phoenix since 1998. Our office provides general dentistry for the whole family in a comfortable, friendly setting. We are a small practice and we believe that dental care should be personal, honest, and never rushed.

We offer routine cleanings and exams, digital x-rays, tooth-colored fillings, crowns and bridges, extractions, dentures and partials, and teeth whitening. New patients are always welcome.
