About Our Practice

Sunrise Family Dental opened its doors in 1998 and has been a north Phoenix fixture ever since. The practice is owned and operated by Dr. Ellen Vargas, DDS, who completed her dental training at the University of Arizona and has practiced general dentistry in the Phoenix area for over twenty years.

Dr. Vargas is joined by Marcy Cole, RDH, our registered dental hygienist, and by our office manager Teresa Nunez, who most patients speak with first when they call.

We are a small, independent practice. We are not part of a corporate dental group, and we think that shows in the way we treat our patients.
